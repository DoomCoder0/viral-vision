Common Questions

How Quickly Will We See Results?
Paid campaigns can start attracting traffic soon after launch. Consistent performance takes time to build through audience testing, creative experimentation and ongoing improvements. The timeline depends on your industry, budget and sales cycle.
How Much Media Budget Do We Need?
There is no universal minimum. We recommend a realistic budget based on your objectives, target audience, market and the amount of data needed to learn and optimize.
Which Advertising Platforms Do You Manage?
Depending on your audience and goals, we can develop strategies across Google Ads, YouTube, Meta, Instagram, TikTok and other relevant platforms.
Can You Improve Our Existing Campaigns?
Yes. We can review your current setup, keep what already works and improve areas such as targeting, conversion tracking, creative and campaign structure.
How Do You Measure Performance?
We define meaningful metrics together: qualified leads, sales, acquisition costs, return on ad spend and the outcomes that connect marketing to your business.
